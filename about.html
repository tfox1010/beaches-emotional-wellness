Meet Tommy Fox.

A real conversation. A more grounded way forward.

LOCAL ROOTS, PERSONAL APPROACH

Support that feels human.

I grew up in Atlantic Beach, and I know how important community, relationships, and a sense of belonging can be. In counseling, I aim to create a space where you feel comfortable being yourself and speaking honestly.

My approach is collaborative and person-centered. Together, we can explore anxiety, difficult transitions, relationship challenges, or the feeling that you're stuck—and identify meaningful steps forward.

My clinical work includes adults, couples, teen boys and young men, and athletes navigating performance pressure and emotional challenges.

Tommy Fox, LMHC
Florida Licensed Mental Health Counselor

WHAT WORKING TOGETHER CAN LOOK LIKE

Warmth, honesty, and practical progress.

No two people come to counseling for exactly the same reason. We'll start by understanding what matters to you, then work together on an approach suited to your goals and needs. Some people are looking for symptom relief; others want to strengthen relationships, handle pressure, or navigate a new chapter.

In-person sessions are available in Ponte Vedra Beach, and virtual therapy is available to clients located in Florida.
