COMMON QUESTIONS

Do you accept insurance?

No. The practice is private pay. If applicable, you can ask about documentation for out-of-network benefits, but reimbursement is not guaranteed.

Do you offer virtual therapy?

Yes. Telehealth is offered for clients physically located in Florida, subject to clinical appropriateness and availability.

Where are in-person sessions held?

In Ponte Vedra Beach, Florida. Confirm the exact office suite when booking.

Do you work with teens?

Yes, individual counseling for boys ages 15+ and young men. The Athlete Edge program may serve athletes ages 13+. Appropriate consent and screening apply.

How do I become a client?

Use the existing secure scheduling portal to request an appointment. Existing clients can continue using the client login.

Are evening or weekend appointments available?

Some may be offered by request, depending on current capacity.

What if I need immediate help?

This website and scheduling portal are not emergency services. In immediate danger, call 911. For crisis support in the U.S., call or text 988.

NO SURPRISES ACT

Good Faith Estimate information

If you do not have insurance or do not plan to use insurance, you generally have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of expected charges for non-emergency health care services. Ask the practice for an estimate and additional information before scheduling or receiving care.
