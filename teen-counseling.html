TEENS & YOUNG MEN

A place for teenage boys to talk, grow, and find their footing.

Counseling for boys ages 15+ and young men managing pressure, anxiety, confidence, family challenges, and change.

Explore appointment times ↗

Growing up isn't always straightforward.

Teenage years bring a lot of change. Expectations from family, school, friends, and sports can become hard to carry. Some teens withdraw; others show frustration or have trouble explaining what's going on.

My goal is to create a respectful, supportive space where young people can talk without feeling judged and develop a better understanding of themselves.

Areas of support

  • Anxiety, stress, and social pressures
  • Confidence and self-understanding
  • Family communication and relationships
  • Managing difficult emotions
  • School, friendships, and major transitions
